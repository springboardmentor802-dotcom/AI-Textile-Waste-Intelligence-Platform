# Textile Waste Intelligence Platform

## Problem Statement

The textile industry generates a huge amount of waste every year. Most textile waste is manually sorted, making recycling slow, expensive, and inaccurate.

## Proposed Solution

The Textile Waste Intelligence Platform uses Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision to automatically identify textile materials, classify waste, estimate recyclability, and recommend recycling or reuse methods.

## Objectives

- Reduce textile waste
- Improve recycling efficiency
- Automate waste classification
- Support sustainable fashion
- Provide environmental impact analysis

## Target Users

- Recycling Facilities
- Textile Manufacturers
- Sustainability Managers
- Administrators

## Expected Outcome

An AI-powered web application capable of helping industries make better decisions for textile waste management.
